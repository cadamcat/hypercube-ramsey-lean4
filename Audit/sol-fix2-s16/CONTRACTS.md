## Existing declaration changes

| Declaration | Old → new; paper reason | Attack and result |
|---|---|---|
| `AvoidanceHypotheses` | Full outside avoidance identity → identity for every R disjoint from the singleton touching family. T16:350–360; A:110–114. | Reused the six-atom counterexample's outside family R={f}. Its true-pin left side is 1/100−1/132=2/825; the required independent right side is (1/100)(131/132)=131/13200. It is rejected. Product group/bin variables supply the stronger identity, including when singleton queries are not allowed. |
| `PermissionLossHypotheses` | Incoming cap 2/B → exp(cperm*n/2)/B; remove group/incidence nonemptiness. T16:138–156; X:155–171. | Markov and the incidence ratio remove at most exp(−cperm*n) of bins. The new cap loses at most exp(−cperm*n/2) incoming mass, so PermissionLossFact can choose c=cperm/2. Empty incidence families permit every bin; empty groups make the row conclusions vacuous. Bin/label nonemptiness remains for denominators. |
| `FreshLabelCalibration` | Encoder label identity on every input → identity only on typical pools and positive history/bin/label sampling support. T16:117–120,294–296,469–482; X:330–339. | The old identity sends constant labels on two different odd roles into a state with equal labels, contradicting universal `FreshCell.Spec.labels_injective`. Guarding it allows invalid encodings to use an injective fallback and retains every input used by fresh-law integration. |
| `FreshPriorPipeline` | Raw-prior and label encoder identities likewise restricted to actual supported sampling inputs. T16:513–529; X:595–604. | The same constant-label input defeats the old unconditional label identity. Invalid raw inputs no longer constrain the fallback's prior. Positive fresh mass still retains the exact raw posterior. |
| `FreshPriorSourceValid` | Inline recLaw/refLaw joint expectation → the named `solverBasePriorExperiment` joint event. T16:527–529; X:683–703. | Expanding FinLaw.bind gives the same joint probability of records and neighbor labels; the event retains both coordinates. The prior readout remains the solver's σ. This uses the previously uncalled endpoint. |

The refuted `avoidance_relative_singletons` theorem itself keeps its binders and conclusion; its input certificate has been repaired. Its body remains a placeholder. `GroupBinHypotheses`, `RoleLabelHypotheses`, and `CellPoolDiagnostics` retain their conditional meanings and now have separate physical producer contracts. The two role failure endpoints are used in `successful_role_label_hypotheses` rather than deleted.
